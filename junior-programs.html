The Journey

Development Pathway

A clear, structured progression from beginner to competitive player.

1

Discover & Play

Introduction to the game through fun, play-based activities. Building basic motor skills, hand-eye coordination, and etiquette. No experience needed.

2

Build Foundations

Developing consistent swing mechanics, short game fundamentals, and understanding course management. Introduction to TrackMan data.

3

Compete & Grow

Entering local and regional tournaments. Developing pre-shot routines, pressure management, and competitive mindset with mental game coaching.

4

Elite Performance

National and international tournament preparation. Advanced biomechanics, fitness integration, college scholarship guidance, and touring professional pathways.

FAQ

Common Questions

Does my child need their own clubs?

No — we have junior-sized clubs available for all ages. As they progress, we can advise on the right equipment to purchase.

Can parents watch the sessions?

Absolutely. Our studio has a comfortable viewing area with refreshments. Parents are always welcome to observe.

My child has never played golf — is that okay?

Perfect! The Little Links program is designed for complete beginners. We make the first experience fun and pressure-free.

How do you handle different skill levels in group sessions?

Groups are kept small (max 4) and are organized by age and ability. Each child gets individual attention within the group format.
