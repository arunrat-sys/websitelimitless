Technology

Powered by the Best

Our bays feature the same launch monitor technology used on the PGA Tour, delivering unmatched accuracy and detail.

TrackMan 4

Dual-radar technology measuring 30+ parameters including club path, face angle, attack angle, spin rate, and ball speed — with accuracy within 1%.

  • 30+ data points per swing
  • Real-time shot trajectory
  • Compare sessions over time

Foresight GCQuad

Quadroscopic camera system capturing club and ball data at impact with photographic precision — the gold standard for club fitting and practice.

  • Camera-based accuracy
  • Club fitting ready
  • Impact location mapping
