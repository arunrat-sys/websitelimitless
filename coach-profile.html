Biography

David Mitchell began his golf journey at the age of 8 in Melbourne, Australia. After competing on the mini-tour circuit across Southeast Asia and Australia for six years, he transitioned into coaching — where he discovered his true calling.

With over 20 years of teaching experience, David has developed a coaching philosophy grounded in biomechanics, data analysis, and player psychology. He was among the first coaches in Thailand to become a certified TrackMan Master, and has since trained over 500 students ranging from absolute beginners to touring professionals.

As Head Professional at Limitless Golf Studio, David oversees all coaching programs, mentors the coaching team, and personally works with elite and junior competitive players. His students have won national championships and represented Thailand in international events.
